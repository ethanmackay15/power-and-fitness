Principles to remember

  • You're close, not endless. ~8 lb of fat from 16.3% → 12%. This board exists so you can see the finish line and the rest-stops — not grind blind.
  • Maintenance islands are scheduled, not emergencies. Your cut wasn't 14 clean weeks (the work-stress sugar stretch broke it up), so the blue blocks are proactive — they discharge diet fatigue and double as your best lean-reclamation windows (140→147 lean even in a deficit).
